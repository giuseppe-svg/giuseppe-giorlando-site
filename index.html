01 / ABOUT

I build ecosystems.
Between people, markets and waves.

Born in Sicily, shaped by Europe. For more than 8 years I have helped global tech brands enter and grow in Italy, Spain and Portugal through partnerships, channel programs and system integrators. I opened the Italian market for BigCommerce, then founded G2Market, a Partnerships-as-a-Service boutique, and Wanderer, the platform for spiritual and holistic experiences. Christian and anthroposophist. I have lived in Málaga, Strasbourg, Pavia, Amsterdam, Dublin, Milan, Buenos Aires and Rio de Janeiro, plus Peru, Bolivia, India and Japan. Off the laptop: microphones, DJ decks, surfboards, dive tanks, a snowboard and a backpack. The sea is where everything makes sense.
