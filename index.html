About

I build ecosystems between people, markets and waves.

I'm Giuseppe Giorlando, a Partnership & Sales leader born in Sicily and shaped by Europe. For more than 8 years I have helped global tech brands enter and grow in Italy, Spain and Portugal through partnerships, channel programs and system integrators. I opened the Italian market for BigCommerce, then founded G2Market, a Partnerships-as-a-Service boutique, and Wanderer, the platform for spiritual and holistic experiences. I am a Christian and an anthroposophist. I have lived in Málaga, Strasbourg, Pavia, Amsterdam, Dublin, Milan, Buenos Aires and Rio de Janeiro, plus Peru, Bolivia, India and Japan. Away from the laptop you will find me with microphones, DJ decks, surfboards, dive tanks, a snowboard and a backpack. The sea is where everything makes sense.

About Giuseppe Giorlando

Who is Giuseppe Giorlando?

Giuseppe Giorlando is a Sicilian entrepreneur and Partnership & Sales leader. He founded Wanderer, the platform for spiritual and holistic events, and G2Market, a Partnerships-as-a-Service firm for SaaS and e-commerce vendors in Italy, Spain and Portugal.

What does Giuseppe Giorlando do?

He builds partner ecosystems and sales channels for tech companies in Southern Europe, after roles at BigCommerce, Salesforce and Heineken. He is also an author, podcaster (Made in Sicily, Chiacchiere Milionarie) and psytrance DJ known as Giorlyx.

What books has Giuseppe Giorlando written?

The Message of the Amanita Muscaria: The Secret of Life, and How I Healed My ACL Naturally, Without Surgery, both available on Amazon.
